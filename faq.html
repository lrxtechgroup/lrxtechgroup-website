Products & account

What is LRX One?

LRX One is LRX Tech Group's product suite, currently comprising LRX One Hive (an intelligent enterprise operating system for workflow automation, AI, analytics, and document management) and LRX One Billing (ZAR-native subscription billing infrastructure). One account gets you into every LRX One product through a single sign-in.

Is LRX One available yet?

Both LRX One Hive and LRX One Billing are marked "Coming Soon." Register your interest from either product's page and we'll be in touch as soon as it's ready to onboard your business.

Do I need a separate account for each product?

No - one LRX One account gets you access to every product in the suite through a single sign-in. Each product is billed separately, but you don't need to register or log in separately for each one.

Pricing & billing

How does billing work across products?

Each product in the LRX One suite is billed separately, at the pricing tier you choose for that product - see the LRX One Hive or LRX One Billing pricing pages for current plans and rates.

What payment methods do you support?

Subscriptions are billed via PayFast, supporting South African EFT and card payments.

Do you offer a free trial or free tier?

Not currently - all of our plans are paid subscription plans. Each product's pricing page breaks down exactly what's included at every tier so you know what you're signing up for before you start.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like, for any reason, from your account's billing settings or by emailing billing@lrxtechgroup.com - no minimum term and no early-cancellation fee. Full details are in our Cancellation Policy.

What's your refund policy?

Fees already paid are generally non-refundable, in line with standard subscription billing practice - the one exception is if you're charged for a period starting after your cancellation already took effect, which we refund in full. See our Refund Policy for the complete picture.

Data & compliance

Where is my data hosted?

Our infrastructure is hosted in AWS South Africa and EU regions. Where any of our third-party processors handle data outside those regions as part of their own service delivery, we rely on contractual safeguards consistent with POPIA's cross-border transfer requirements. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

Is LRX One POPIA compliant?

Yes - our products are built and operated in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). See our Privacy Policy for what we collect, why, and the rights you have over your information.

Who owns the data I put into LRX One?

You do. You retain ownership of the business data, documents, and content you submit - we're only licensed to host, process, and display it as needed to provide the service, as set out in our Terms of Service.
